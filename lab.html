How it works

Steps are peaks in vertical acceleration. A turn is 30° or more of rotation about the vertical axis while walking, read from the gyroscope, so it works however you hold the phone. The route is a list like “walk 24 steps, turn left, walk 10 steps”. Retrace reverses the list and flips each turn, then counts your steps down and watches for the turn. Step counts are only a rough guide, so the app shows a range rather than a single number, warns you when the turn is near, and keeps waiting if you walk past the count. A turn you actually make is trusted over the count: it advances the directions wherever you make it, except in the first few steps of a long stretch. After the first turn the app measures how your return pace compares with the recording and scales the remaining counts to match. Vibration and spoken cues fire when a turn is coming up, when you reach it, and on arrival. Not a validated navigation tool.
